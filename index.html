AURALIS × TEENAGE ENG.

Auralis Alpha: Tactile Acoustic Hardware

CHALLENGE Bridge raw acoustic vacuum tubes with digital touch controls.
TIMELINE 14 Weeks Sprint
MEASURED LIFT +240% Pre-Order Velocity

The Conceptual Brief

Auralis challenged Vanguard to create a high-fidelity audio system that defied modern black-plastic conventions. We opted for architectural frosted glass, dark titanium knobs with magnetically variable detents, and synthetic acoustic feedback generated directly by physical user manipulation.

Technical Execution

We developed a real-time WebGL configurator paired with embedded micro-firmware UI. Custom GLSL shaders replicated internal light refraction across 16 layers of frosted silicate glass.

Deliverables

  • ✦ Custom Industrial Form Factor
  • ✦ Embedded Display Micro-Firmware
  • ✦ WebGL Interactive Launch Engine
  • ✦ Tactile Packaging & Typography
